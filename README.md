# CarbonLoop 🌱

### GIS-Powered Waste-to-Carbon-Value Chain Tracker

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-CarbonLoop-2ea44f?style=for-the-badge)](https://carbon-loop-eight.vercel.app/)

**CarbonLoop** is a GIS-powered platform that connects waste generators with carbon-conversion facilities, optimizes waste transportation, calculates measurable CO₂e benefits, and records the resulting environmental and economic value in a carbon ledger.

The platform addresses the **Waste-to-Carbon-Value Chain Tracker** problem by turning organic and industrial waste from a landfill liability into a measurable carbon and economic asset.

## 🎯 Problem

Large quantities of organic and industrial waste from farms, food industries, and municipalities are sent to landfills despite having potential for conversion into:

- Biochar
- Biogas
- Biomaterials
- Compost
- Other carbon-negative or carbon-beneficial products

This creates three major problems:

1. Valuable waste resources are lost.
2. Landfill emissions are generated unnecessarily.
3. Waste generators and conversion facilities lack an efficient way to discover and coordinate suitable matches.

CarbonLoop addresses this by creating a connected digital workflow from **waste generation → facility matching → route optimization → carbon calculation → economic valuation**.
